# Interview guide

**Duration:** 15–20 minutes. **Format:** call or text chat. Read the [consent text](consent.md) first and record the answer.

## Questions

1. How long have you been making memes, and where do you publish them?
2. Walk me through the last meme you made about a news story. How long did it take from seeing the news to publishing?
3. Have you tried AI tools for memes? What worked and what didn't?
4. Have you ever worried that a meme could misrepresent the news it is about? What happened?
5. Do you earn anything from memes now? How, and what stops you from earning more?
6. If a platform paid you a share of ad revenue and subscriptions, what would you need to trust it? Would you pass identity verification to get paid?
7. How would you prefer to be paid (bank, PayPal, crypto, stablecoin)? Why?
